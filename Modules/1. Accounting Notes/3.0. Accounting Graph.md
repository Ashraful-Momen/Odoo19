অবশ্যই। তুমি যেহেতু **Odoo/ERP Accounting** শিখছো, আমি এমন একটা ASCII map দিচ্ছি যেটা দেখে **Account Type → Increase → Decrease → Dr/Cr** সহজে মনে রাখতে পারবে।

## 🧠 Master Accounting Map

```text
                         ACCOUNTING
                             │
              ┌──────────────┴──────────────┐
              │                             │
            DEBIT                         CREDIT
              │                             │
       "What comes in?"              "What goes out?"
       "Expense increases"           "Income increases"
       "Asset increases"             "Liability increases"
              │                             │
              └──────────────┬──────────────┘
                             │
                    ┌────────┴────────┐
                    │                 │
                 ASSETS             CLAIMS
                    │                 │
              ┌─────┴─────┐      ┌────┴─────┐
              │           │      │          │
            Asset       Expense  Liability  Equity
              │           │      │          │
             DR↑         DR↑    CR↑        CR↑
             CR↓         CR↓    DR↓        DR↓
                                      │
                                    Income
                                      │
                                     CR↑
                                     DR↓
```

কিন্তু এটাকে আরও সহজে মনে রাখার জন্য **এই 5টা rule** মুখস্থ করো।

# 🔥 The Golden 5 Rules

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              ACCOUNTING RULE                 │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                              │
│  ASSET       →  DR ↑   | CR ↓               │
│  EXPENSE     →  DR ↑   | CR ↓               │
│                                              │
│  LIABILITY   →  CR ↑   | DR ↓               │
│  EQUITY      →  CR ↑   | DR ↓               │
│  INCOME      →  CR ↑   | DR ↓               │
│                                              │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

অর্থাৎ:

```text
              INCREASE
                 │
        ┌────────┴────────┐
        │                 │
       DR                CR
        │                 │
   Asset ↑           Liability ↑
   Expense ↑         Equity ↑
                     Income ↑
```

---

# 1️⃣ Asset

Asset মানে company-এর **যা আছে/যার value আছে**।

```text
ASSET
 │
 ├── Cash
 ├── Bank
 ├── Petty Cash
 ├── Inventory
 ├── Accounts Receivable
 ├── Equipment
 ├── Building
 └── Vehicle

          Asset
            │
       ┌────┴────┐
       │         │
      DR ↑      CR ↓
```

### Example

Customer তোমাকে ৳10,000 দিল।

```text
Cash                 DR 10,000
    Sales Revenue        CR 10,000
```

Cash = Asset → **DR**

---

# 2️⃣ Expense

Company-এর খরচ।

```text
EXPENSE
 │
 ├── Salary
 ├── Rent
 ├── Electricity
 ├── Transport
 ├── Internet
 ├── Office Expense
 └── Insurance Expense

          Expense
             │
        ┌────┴────┐
        │         │
       DR ↑      CR ↓
```

Example:

Office rent ৳20,000 paid:

```text
Rent Expense         DR 20,000
    Cash                 CR 20,000
```

**Expense বাড়লে → DR**

---

# 3️⃣ Liability

Company-এর **দেনা/obligation**।

```text
LIABILITY
 │
 ├── Accounts Payable
 ├── Loan
 ├── Accrued Expense
 ├── VAT Payable
 ├── Tax Payable
 └── Bank Loan

        Liability
             │
        ┌────┴────┐
        │         │
       DR ↓      CR ↑
```

Example:

Supplier থেকে ৳50,000-এর product বাকিতে কিনলে:

```text
Inventory            DR 50,000
    Accounts Payable     CR 50,000
```

Supplier-এর কাছে তোমার **দেনা বাড়ল** → Liability ↑ → **CR**

---

# 4️⃣ Equity

Owner-এর company-তে ownership/value।

```text
EQUITY
 │
 ├── Owner Capital
 ├── Retained Earnings
 └── Current Year Profit

         Equity
            │
       ┌────┴────┐
       │         │
      DR ↓      CR ↑
```

Owner company-তে ৳1,00,000 capital দিল:

```text
Bank                 DR 100,000
    Owner Capital        CR 100,000
```

Capital = Equity → **CR**

---

# 5️⃣ Income / Revenue

Company-এর আয়।

```text
INCOME / REVENUE
 │
 ├── Sales Revenue
 ├── Service Revenue
 ├── Commission Income
 └── Interest Income

          Income
             │
        ┌────┴────┐
        │         │
       DR ↓      CR ↑
```

Product বিক্রি করলে:

```text
Cash                 DR 10,000
    Sales Revenue        CR 10,000
```

Revenue বাড়ল → **CR**

---

# ⭐ সবচেয়ে গুরুত্বপূর্ণ Shortcut

এইটা মুখস্থ করো:

```text
              DR
              │
        ┌─────┴─────┐
        │           │
      ASSET       EXPENSE
        ↑           ↑
       DR          DR


              CR
              │
     ┌────────┼────────┐
     │        │        │
 LIABILITY  EQUITY    INCOME
     ↑        ↑         ↑
    CR       CR        CR
```

অর্থাৎ:

> **AE = Debit**
> **LEI = Credit**

### AE → DR

**A = Asset**
**E = Expense**

### LEI → CR

**L = Liability**
**E = Equity**
**I = Income**

এটা মনে রাখলে 80–90% basic Dr/Cr ভুল হবে না।

# 🧩 এখন Journal Entry বুঝো

প্রতিটা transaction-এ সাধারণত:

```text
             TRANSACTION
                  │
                  ↓
           কোন Account?
                  │
          ┌───────┴───────┐
          ↓               ↓
       Account 1       Account 2
          │               │
       Increase?        Increase?
          │               │
          ↓               ↓
        DR/CR           DR/CR
```

### Example 1 — Cash Sale

তুমি ৳10,000-এর product cash-এ বিক্রি করলে।

দুইটা account:

```text
Cash          → Asset ↑ → DR
Sales         → Income ↑ → CR
```

Journal:

```text
Cash                  DR 10,000
    Sales Revenue         CR 10,000
```

---

### Example 2 — Salary Paid

৳30,000 salary দিলে:

```text
Salary Expense    → Expense ↑ → DR
Cash              → Asset ↓   → CR
```

```text
Salary Expense         DR 30,000
    Cash                   CR 30,000
```

---

### Example 3 — Loan Received

Bank থেকে ৳5 লাখ loan নিলে:

```text
Bank              → Asset ↑     → DR
Bank Loan         → Liability ↑ → CR
```

```text
Bank                  DR 500,000
    Bank Loan             CR 500,000
```

---

### Example 4 — Customer বাকিতে কিনল

৳20,000-এর product customer বাকিতে নিল:

```text
Accounts Receivable → Asset ↑  → DR
Sales Revenue       → Income ↑ → CR
```

```text
Accounts Receivable     DR 20,000
    Sales Revenue           CR 20,000
```

Customer এখন তোমার কাছে **দেনাদার**।

---

### Example 5 — Supplier-এর টাকা পরিশোধ

Supplier-এর ৳20,000 payable ছিল, payment করে দিলে:

```text
Accounts Payable → Liability ↓ → DR
Cash             → Asset ↓     → CR
```

```text
Accounts Payable      DR 20,000
    Cash                  CR 20,000
```

# 🔥 Odoo-এর জন্য এই Map-টা খুব গুরুত্বপূর্ণ

Odoo-তে Account Type দেখলে এভাবে classify করবে:

```text
ACCOUNT
   │
   ├── ASSET
   │     ├── Bank
   │     ├── Cash
   │     ├── Petty Cash
   │     ├── Receivable
   │     └── Inventory
   │
   ├── LIABILITY
   │     ├── Payable
   │     ├── Loan
   │     └── Tax Payable
   │
   ├── EQUITY
   │     ├── Capital
   │     └── Retained Earnings
   │
   ├── INCOME
   │     ├── Sales
   │     └── Service Revenue
   │
   └── EXPENSE
         ├── Salary
         ├── Rent
         ├── Electricity
         └── Purchase/Operating Expense
```

## 🧠 শেষের Super Shortcut

```text
                 ACCOUNTING
                     │
          ┌──────────┴──────────┐
          │                     │
       DEBIT ↑              CREDIT ↑
          │                     │
     ┌────┴────┐          ┌─────┴─────┐
     │         │          │     │     │
   ASSET    EXPENSE    LIABILITY EQUITY INCOME
```

**শুধু এই লাইনটা মনে রাখো:**

> 🟢 **Asset + Expense → Increase = DR**
> 🔵 **Liability + Equity + Income → Increase = CR**

আর **decrease হলে ঠিক উল্টো side**।

এটাই তোমার **Odoo Journal Entry, Vendor Bill, Customer Invoice, Payment, Bank, Petty Cash, AR/AP**—সবকিছুর foundation।
